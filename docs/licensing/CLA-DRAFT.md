# Contributor license agreement — draft, not activated

This is a review draft for the intended EMCargo licensor. It is not an agreement
contributors have accepted. Before activation, identify that legal person,
obtain legal review of the final text, choose a signature/record system and
publish an effective date. Existing contributions require separate consent
where additional rights are needed. No consent is inferred retroactively.

## Proposed terms

1. **Parties and scope.** The contributor and the legal entity or individual
   identified in the signature record as the EMCargo licensor enter this
   agreement. A Contribution is the contributor's original material explicitly
   submitted for inclusion in EMCargo and identified in that record. Material
   clearly marked as excluded or owned by another party is not included.
2. **Copyright retained.** The contributor retains ownership. The contributor
   grants the licensor a worldwide, perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
   irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, modify, prepare derivative
   works, display, perform and distribute the Contribution, and to sublicense
   those rights under the current project terms or different terms, including
   commercial licenses. This is a license, not an assignment of ownership.
3. **Patent grant.** For patent claims the contributor can license that are
   necessarily infringed by the Contribution alone or its intended combination
   with EMCargo, the contributor grants the licensor and recipients a worldwide,
   non-exclusive, royalty-free patent license to make, use, offer, sell, import
   and otherwise transfer that combination. Counsel must finalise the scope and
   patent-litigation termination provision before activation.
4. **Authority.** The contributor represents that they have authority to grant
   these rights. If an employer or another person holds relevant rights, obtain
   its authorisation before submission and identify it in the private record.
   Disclose third-party material and its terms separately. Do not offer someone
   else's regulatory publication, form, dataset, logo or code as your own.
5. **No obligation or warranty.** The project need not use a Contribution. It is
   supplied without a support obligation and without warranties to the extent
   permitted by law. Applicable mandatory rights remain unaffected.
6. **Attribution and moral rights.** Preserve existing required notices. Counsel
   must define permitted attribution and adaptation consistently with any
   non-waivable moral rights; this draft does not purport to waive them globally.
7. **Acceptance record.** Record the final agreement version and hash, legal
   names of both parties, contributor account, relevant contributions,
   authority where applicable, date and affirmative signature. Store personal
   records privately; a public check should expose only accepted/pending and
   the agreement version. Final governing law and dispute terms remain open.

## Activation checklist

- Identify the licensor and settle ownership questions in `license-history.md`.
- Finalise patent, attribution, governing-law and mandatory-law provisions.
- Implement affirmative acceptance bound to a particular text and contributor;
  test amended PRs, organisations and revocation/termination handling.
- Update CONTRIBUTING and the effective date only after this process exists.

A DCO can document contribution provenance but does not by itself provide the
broader commercial sublicensing rights contemplated here. The existing inbound
terms remain in force until a completed agreement is activated.
