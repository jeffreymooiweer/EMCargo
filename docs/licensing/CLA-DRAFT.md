# Contributor license agreement — draft, not activated

Draft 0.2, 2026-09-15. These are proposed terms, not current inbound terms.
Publication, an issue or a patch does not constitute acceptance. Identify the
licensor and implement the acceptance process before activation. No existing
contributor is deemed to have accepted additional rights retrospectively.

## 1. Parties and contributions

The parties are the Contributor and the EMCargo Licensor identified by legal
name in the acceptance record. An organisation acts through an authorised
representative; an account name alone does not identify the contracting party.
A Contribution is original code, documentation, translation or other material
intentionally submitted for inclusion in EMCargo and identified by repository,
pull request and commit or file hash in that record. Future submissions are
covered only if prospective coverage is expressly selected and each submission
is intentionally made for inclusion under this agreement. Clearly identified
third-party or excluded material is outside the grants below.

## 2. Ownership and copyright license

The Contributor retains ownership and may use and license the Contribution
elsewhere. The Contributor grants the Licensor a worldwide, perpetual,
non-exclusive, royalty-free copyright license to reproduce, adapt, translate,
combine, display, perform, make available and distribute the Contribution and
derivative works, and to sublicense these acts through multiple tiers. Subject
to mandatory law, this grant is irrevocable.

Sublicenses may use the present project license or different terms, including
proprietary and paid commercial terms, and may cover hosting, distribution and
integration. No further payment or consent is required for acts within this
grant. It covers only rights the Contributor can grant; it does not relicense
third-party code, datasets or forms.

## 3. Limited patent license

For patent claims the Contributor owns or controls and can license without
payment to another party, the Contributor grants the Licensor and recipients
a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to make, have made, use, offer
for sale, sell, import and transfer the Contribution. This covers only claims
necessarily infringed by the Contribution itself or its combination with the
EMCargo work to which it was submitted, not unrelated products or claims
introduced solely by later modifications.

If a recipient initiates patent litigation alleging infringement by the
Contribution or that submitted combination, the patent license granted to that
recipient under this agreement ends when litigation is initiated. This does
not terminate unrelated licenses or enlarge the grant. Mandatory limitations
on this clause remain applicable.

## 4. Authority and provenance

The Contributor confirms authority to make these grants. If an employer,
client or other party holds relevant rights, that holder must sign or give
express written authority covering these grants. An individual's representation
cannot replace rights held by someone else. Identify third-party material,
licenses, notices, source editions and restrictions separately, and disclose
known conflicts affecting the grants. Notify the Licensor promptly if a
material authority statement proves inaccurate.

Disclose AI assistance where relevant to provenance. This agreement neither
creates copyright in unprotected output nor clears copied third-party material.

## 5. Attribution and moral rights

The Licensor preserves required notices and, where reasonably practicable,
records the Contributor's chosen public name in the contribution history.
There is no promise of attribution on every screen or generated document.
The Contributor consents to adaptations, translations, combinations and
ordinary editorial changes within the grant, to the extent legally effective.
No non-waivable moral rights are waived. False authorship or endorsement is
not permitted, and no trademark rights are granted.

## 6. No acceptance, payment or support obligation

The Licensor need not accept, publish or maintain a Contribution. This agreement
promises no employment, payment, future work or support service. Apart from the
express authority statements, the Contribution is supplied without contractual
warranty to the extent permitted by law. This does not excuse fraud, override
mandatory liability or warrant transport compliance or safety.

## 7. Duration and changes

Grants for identified Contributions survive the end of participation. A
Contributor may end prospective coverage by written notice before a future
submission; that does not retract grants already made, subject to mandatory
law and the patent clause. Agreement changes require affirmative acceptance
of the new version; a website edit alone does not amend an existing agreement.
Correcting a contribution record does not establish rights never held.

## 8. Governing law and disputes

The proposed agreement is governed by Dutch law, subject to mandatory
protections. Parties first seek a practical resolution through the legal
contacts recorded with acceptance. Failing resolution, jurisdiction follows
applicable procedural law; this draft imposes no exclusive foreign forum or
arbitration. Separate employment, assignment, customer and third-party license
agreements are not replaced by this contribution agreement.

## 9. Affirmative acceptance record

| Field | Required information |
| --- | --- |
| Licensor | Legal name, legal contact and, for an entity, registration and representative details |
| Contributor | Legal name, verified account and organisational authority where relevant |
| Text | Final version, retained complete text and SHA-256 |
| Coverage | Exact contributions and explicit choice about future intentional submissions |
| Exceptions | Third-party material, excluded files and limits of employer/client authority |
| Assent | Dated affirmative signature or equivalent attributable acceptance, with evidence of the presented text |
| Privacy | Purpose, legal basis, restricted access and proportionate retention of identity/authority records |

Proposed acceptance statement: “I accept the EMCargo Contributor License
Agreement identified in this record, for the contributions and coverage stated
here, and confirm my authority to grant the rights described in it.”

Keep identity and authority records private. A public check should expose only
accepted/pending, version and covered contribution identifiers. Added commits,
changed authors, organisational authority and agreement versions require a new
coverage check; an earlier green check does not cover another author's work.
