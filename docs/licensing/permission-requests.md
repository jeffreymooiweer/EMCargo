## Request text

We maintain EMCargo, software with publicly viewable source under Apache License
2.0 with the Commons Clause. We would like to establish the rights applicable
to the attached list of your materials (title, edition, source URL and SHA-256).

Please confirm separately whether we may (a) include the original files in a
public Git repository and software archives/container images, (b) fill or modify
the forms and provide completed PDFs to end users, (c) distribute extracted
data or text and generated UN cards, and (d) use and supply these materials as
part of commercial hosting or support. Please specify attribution, notices,
modification/translation restrictions, sublicensing, territory, duration,
updates, fees and any exclusions. We do not claim your certification or
endorsement.

If those activities require a separate agreement, please identify the correct
licensor and agreement. Local possession or use rights may differ from public
redistribution rights; we would appreciate a clear distinction in your answer.
