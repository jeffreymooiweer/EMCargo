# Commercial licensing preparation

Draft decision framework, 2026-09-15. No commercial offer or additional grant is
made here. The current LICENSE and earlier valid grants remain in place.

## Meaning of the current restriction

The [Commons Clause](https://commonsclause.com/) restricts specified third-party
commercial exploitation where a product or service derives all or a substantial
part of its value from the software. Its definition includes some hosting,
consulting and support arrangements. It is not a general ban on internal
business use or a blanket exemption for paid services. The actual offering
and rights held by the licensor determine the answer.

| Offering | Working assessment | Contracting consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business uses its installation for its own shipments | Ordinary internal use is not the defined sale of EMCargo | Follow software/data terms and the participants' operational duties |
| Third party sells hosted EMCargo access | Likely restricted when the software supplies the substantial value | Obtain a separate grant from parties with sufficient rights |
| Paid installation, modification or support | Fact-dependent; no blanket consulting exemption | Describe deliverables, value and recipients in a written scope |
| Independently implemented similar workflow | Functionality alone is not ownership of an implementation | Assess copied expression, patents, branding and confidentiality separately |
| Valid MIT-era copy | Later terms do not withdraw the earlier grant | Identify the code/version and preserve applicable notices |

## Proposed commercial order form

Complete these fields before making an offer. A GitHub profile is not evidence
that a particular legal entity exists or holds the necessary rights.

| Schedule | Content to agree |
| --- | --- |
| Parties and authority | Legal names, addresses, registration identifiers where applicable, signatories and chain of title |
| Licensed work | Exact release/revision, enterprise modules, exclusions and immutable third-party schedule |
| Grant | Internal use, named hosted service, territories, affiliates, tenants, modification, redistribution and further sublicensing |
| Charges and term | Fee basis, taxes, payment, start/end, renewal and price-change procedure |
| Maintenance | Supported versions/period, updates, security fixes, end-of-life notice and migration duties |
| Service levels | Measured scope, availability method, support hours, response targets, dependencies, maintenance and remedies |
| Customer operations | Input, competent review, operator-supplied templates, backup/restore and access control |
| Data protection | Actual roles, processor terms if applicable, subprocessors, location/transfers, retention, incident assistance and deletion/return |
| Claims and liability | Warranties/remedies, allocated IP risks, lawful exclusions, justified limits and insurance alignment |
| Exit | Export format, transition assistance, retention/deletion, surviving rights and outstanding invoices |
| Branding | Agreed use without implied publisher endorsement or certification |

Suggested grant for review: “For the term and scope identified in the Order,
the Licensor grants the Customer a non-exclusive license to perform the acts
expressly listed in the Order with the identified EMCargo software, including
acts that would otherwise require separate permission under its Commons Clause.
This grant is limited to rights the Licensor holds. Third-party material remains
subject to the separate terms supplied with it.”

Do not promise exclusive rights over historical MIT code or contributions
without sufficient grants. A commercial EMCargo license does not supply
publisher rights for forms or IMDG material. A community/enterprise division
requires an actual product scope and contract, not just a pricing page.

## Existing terms draft

`TERMS.nl.md` remains an unaccepted concept for free professional self-hosting.
Its draft liability amounts are not validated by this research and are not
established insurance limits. Select paid-service limits against the service,
foreseeable losses, mandatory law and actual coverage. Make terms available
for storage before or at contract formation and record an authorised
organisation's assent; a footer link does not prove acceptance by every user.
