These are observations from Git, not a determination that every file in every
revision was owned by the person changing the license. Preserve the original
license notices applicable to each copy. Later terms do not retrospectively
withdraw permissions validly granted for earlier copies. Do not claim that
the current Commons Clause prevents every commercial reuse of historical code.

The current license is a source-available arrangement, not OSI open source.
The Commons Clause defines "Sell" by substantial value derived from the
software and expressly mentions some services. Internal business use is not
automatically prohibited. Whether a specific paid hosting, support, integration
or consulting arrangement needs a separate grant must be assessed against the
actual text and the rights held by its licensor.

Before issuing commercial grants, establish the legal licensor and collect
evidence for employee, contractor and third-party contributions. Record the
scope of any employer agreement or assignment, the relevant commits and the
person authorised to grant rights. Do not publish personnel contracts or
private contributor identity documents in this repository. A sole Git author,
repository administration rights or a DCO sign-off is not proof of ownership.

## Private chain-of-title worksheet

For each substantial component, record its author, creation period, relevant
commits, employment/client relationship at that time, actual assigned duties,
instructions, applicable agreements and the evidence establishing who may
license it. Distinguish a confirmation of existing ownership from an assignment
or broader license; record exact subject matter and authority for either.
Personal development and employment can overlap, but employment alone does not
establish that every private work belongs to the employer. Conversely, working
outside normal hours does not decide ownership of assigned software development.

The Dutch [copyright information for software programmers](https://www.auteursrecht.nl/ik-ben-maker/Software-programmeur)
distinguishes employee-created work from external commissioned software, for
which the customer does not automatically acquire copyright. The employee rule
is also addressed in [Software Directive 2009/24/EC, article 2(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/24/article/2).
National law, the actual duties and agreements determine the individual case.
The linked directive is the archived EU text, not UK law applied to this project.

For AI-assisted changes, retain available provenance and identify human design,
selection and editing rather than assuming every output is independently
protected or free of third-party expression. A new copyright line or CLA does
not create missing rights. Keep employment facts and legal-person records out
of the public repository; record only a non-sensitive disposition/reference.

Copyright protects the program's expression, not functionality as such; the
[CJEU's SAS decision explanation](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2012-05/cp120053en.pdf)
also distinguishes lawful study from reproducing protected source or expression.
Trade secrecy cannot be presumed for code deliberately made public, and patents,
confidential materials and misleading branding require separate analysis.
